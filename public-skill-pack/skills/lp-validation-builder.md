# LP Validation Builder

Use this to test an offer before building a full funnel.

## Rule

One page. One audience. One offer. One CTA.

## Inputs

- Target audience.
- Offer or freebie.
- Pain/problem.
- Desired action: email, LINE, purchase, inquiry, DM.
- Proof or credibility.
- Delivery method.
- Mobile priority.

Never include API keys, passwords, cookies, customer lists, or payment credentials.

## Structure

1. Hero: who it is for, what they get, why it matters.
2. Pain: what feels confusing or risky.
3. Solution: what the freebie/offer helps with.
4. Included items: short list of deliverables.
5. How to use: 3-4 simple steps.
6. Safety/trust: what it does not promise, what is not required.
7. FAQ: beginner objections.
8. CTA: repeat the same primary action.

## Mobile Rules

- Large readable text.
- High contrast.
- Full-width buttons on phones.
- No dense multi-column sections on mobile.
- Short form: email or LINE only.
- Check at 390px and 430px widths.
- Explain what can be done on phone and what is easier on PC.

## Safety Check

- No income guarantees.
- No fake testimonials.
- No exaggerated scarcity.
- Add privacy note before collecting email/LINE.
- Check links.
- Human confirmation for posting, payment, deletion, sending, or automation.

## Reminder
