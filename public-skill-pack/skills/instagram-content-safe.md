# Instagram Content Safe

Use this to create Instagram post ideas without copying viral accounts or exposing private details.

## Inputs

- Topic or real episode.
- Audience.
- Desired reaction: save, comment, share, DM.
- Post type: caption, carousel, Reel idea.

## Safe Content Flow

1. Find the real moment or practical lesson.
2. Remove private details: names, exact locations, school/workplace, identifiable schedules.
3. Choose a format:
   - Relatable story.
   - Checklist.
   - Before/after.
   - Viewer question.
   - Mini experiment.
4. Write a hook.
5. Give one useful idea.
6. End with an easy question or save prompt.

## Viral Format Adaptation

- Disclosure type: show hidden effort, time, cost, or honest result.
- Preparation/making-of: show the process.
- Silent review: show comparison with minimal words.
- Viewer quiz: ask people to guess or choose.
- Observation/snapshot: show approved, anonymized everyday scenes.

## Rules

- Do not copy wording from viral posts.
- Do not use unverified numbers as proof.
- Do not shame family, customers, students, or viewers.
- Avoid excessive fear or income claims.
