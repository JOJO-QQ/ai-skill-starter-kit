# Note Structure Safe

Use this to turn practical experience into a readable article.

## Inputs

- Topic.
- Target reader.
- Free or paid article.
- Real experience, mistake, workflow, or result.
- Desired reader action.

## Structure

```
Title
Opening: specific problem scene
What you will learn: 3 bullets
Why it matters
Actual steps
What went wrong
Safety/cost/settings notes
Checklist or template
Summary: 3 lines
Next action
```

## Title Patterns

- `I tried xxx and learned yyy`
- `Beginner-safe guide to xxx`
- `What I wish I knew before xxx`
- `xxx checklist for non-engineers`
- `How to test xxx without overbuilding`

## Paid Split

Free section:
- Problem.
- Real story.
- Overall flow.
- One useful insight.

Paid section:
- Checklist.
- Copy-paste prompts.
- Templates.
- Screenshots or exact steps.
- Failure recovery.

## Safety Rules

- Do not expose API keys, local paths, account IDs, private filenames, or billing details.
- Explain whether an action costs money.
- Avoid income promises and fear-based hooks.
- Prefer tested steps over claims.
