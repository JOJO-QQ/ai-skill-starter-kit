# Market Research Starter

Use this before making a product or service.

## Inputs

- Idea or niche.
- Target customer.
- Existing strengths.
- Platform candidates.
- What you want to sell.

## Workflow

1. Define one narrow customer.
2. Identify the painful task.
3. Check existing offers on marketplaces, search, SNS, and content platforms.
4. Compare:
   - Price.
   - Deliverables.
   - Proof.
   - FAQ.
   - Support.
   - Reviews or recent activity.
5. Find a smaller first offer.
6. Choose a validation method.

## Rules

- Do not build from imagination only.
- Validate with a small page, sample, post, template, or direct outreach.
- Treat revenue screenshots as marketing unless verified.
- Sell time saved, confusion removed, or risk reduced.
- Prefer a small group that strongly wants the offer over a large audience that only mildly likes it.
- Strong validation signals include replies, saves, repeated use, referrals, direct questions, and willingness to try or pay.
- For landing pages, start with one page and one CTA. Measure clicks, signups, replies, and consultations before building a full funnel.
- Check income claims, testimonials, urgency, affiliate disclosures, privacy notes, and link safety before publishing.
- A validation LP should make target, benefit, and free offer clear in the first view. Use a simple flow: pain -> solution -> what is included -> how to use -> safety/FAQ -> CTA.
- For older or non-technical audiences, use larger text, high contrast, plain language, and a short form.
